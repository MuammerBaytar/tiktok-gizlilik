Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

1. Who this applies to

60saniyedebirkonu ("the App") is a personal tool used only by its owner to publish the owner's own videos to the owner's own TikTok account. It has no other users and does not collect data from visitors of this website.

2. Information the App handles

When the owner authorizes the App through TikTok Login Kit, TikTok provides an access token and a refresh token for the owner's own account, together with basic profile information (username). The App also sends video files and captions that the owner has prepared to TikTok.

3. How information is used and stored

The tokens are used only to publish the owner's videos and are stored on the owner's own computer. They are not shared with, sold to or disclosed to any third party. The App does not collect or store information about any other TikTok user.

4. Retention and deletion

Tokens can be revoked at any time in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy, Security and permissions, Apps and services. Deleting the local token file removes the App's access.
